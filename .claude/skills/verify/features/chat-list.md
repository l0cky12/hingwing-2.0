# Chat list

The Chats column lists the chats of the open chat's bot, newest first. It shows only in chat mode, in the 288 px column left of the chat, under the mode switch and the bot picker (see [Bot mode and chat mode](./modes.md)). Each row is a button named by the chat's title. It shows when the chat was last used, and "Working" or "Waiting for your OK" while the chat runs or waits on an approval, in place of its preview. A click opens that chat, and the top bar's heading names it as its bot and title. Each row has a More button beside it, shown on hover or focus in place of the time, and turned off while the connection is not online. A right-click on a row opens the same menu. Its menu holds "Rename…", "Export as Markdown", "Export as JSON", "Export as HTML", "Copy link", "Archive" and "Remove…". The row of a bot's own chat, its Bot Chat as the roster names it, has no "Archive" or "Remove…". Rename turns the title into a "Chat name" field: Enter saves, and Escape or leaving the field cancels. An export saves the chat and says "Saved <file name>" under the list for five seconds. Archive takes the row out of the list at once and says "Archived <name>" there. Remove asks first, in a dialog that names the chat, with Cancel and Remove. Escape or Cancel closes it onto the row's More button. When the archived or removed chat was the open one, the pane moves to its bot's own chat, unstarted if it has none, and focus moves to the next row. A refusal shows its words under the row. A bot with no chats says "No chats yet." The column follows the open chat to another bot, picked with the bot picker. It is hidden below 680 px of window width.

## Sub-features

- `chat-list-rows` lists a bot's chats by title when the bot is opened.
- `chat-list-open` opens a chat picked from the list, marks its row current, and shows its thread.
- `chat-list-asking` says "Waiting for your OK" on the row of a chat that waits on an approval while another chat is open.
- `chat-list-runs-in` runs a message in a picked chat as its bot, in that chat's session, and leaves the other chats as they were.
- `chat-list-heading` names the open chat in the top bar's heading.
- `chat-list-empty` says "No chats yet." for a bot with no chats, with the New chat button still in the top bar.
- `chat-list-rename` renames a chat from its More menu, in the row and in the top bar. Escape and leaving the field send nothing.
- `chat-list-export` exports a chat as Markdown from its More menu with the keyboard, saves the file, and names it under the list. The More button never opens the chat.
- `chat-list-offline` turns every More button off while the connection is down.
- `chat-list-archive` archives a chat that is not open from its More menu. The row leaves, the open chat stays, and the list Hermes sends after leaves it out.
- `chat-list-archive-open` archives the open chat. The pane shows the bot's unstarted chat, and a message there makes its Bot Chat.
- `chat-list-remove-cancel` opens the Remove dialog and closes it with Cancel and with Escape, sending nothing.
- `chat-list-remove` removes the open chat after the dialog, with the chat's generation, and the pane moves as for an archive.
- `chat-list-remove-refused` says why a chat whose reply still runs can't be removed, under its row, and asks Hermes nothing.
- `chat-list-bot-chat` offers no Archive or Remove on a Bot Chat's row.
- `chat-list-right-click` opens the row's menu on a right-click with no desktop menu over it, and walks the longer menu with the keys.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Turn on chat mode from the switch at the top of the left column. Click the bot picker at its right and pick a bot. Its chats show in the Chats column.
- Click a chat's row, or move to it with Tab and press Enter.
- Hover a row, or Tab to its More button, and press it. Arrow keys, Home and End move through the menu, and Enter picks.

## Gotchas

- An ARIA snapshot does not print `aria-current`. Use `current button "<title>"`.
- A row's name is its title alone. The ARIA snapshot prints the rest of the row after the name: the time, then the state words or the preview.
- The seeded approval is never answered. The gateway has no handler for a seeded chat's `approval.respond`.
- The time on each row is counted from the machine's clock, so a seeded chat reads some days old.
- `called` is checked again when the steps end, so a count of 0 for a call the run later makes fails. Prove a cancel in a run of its own.
- The scripted gateway takes a `rename`, `archive` and `delete` mutate and `session.export` for a seeded chat. Its export holds only the title and the messages. An archived chat leaves its stored list and a removed one every list, as Hermes does.
- The status line says "Archived <name>" and "Removed <name>", so `absent` of a chat's name fails while it shows. Count the row's button instead.
- The Chats column is in the page only in chat mode, and hidden below 680 px. A run in bot mode, or with a narrower `--size`, has no `navigation "Chats"`.
