# Chat list

The Chats column lists the chats of the open chat's bot, newest first. The chats the user pinned come first, under the heading "Pinned", then the rest under "Sessions", then the snoozed ones under "Snoozed". While neither Pinned nor Snoozed holds a chat there are no headings. Hermes pins each bot's own chat itself, and that chat stays under "Sessions". It shows only in chat mode, in the 288 px column left of the chat, under the mode switch and the bot picker (see [Bot mode and chat mode](./modes.md)). Each row is a button named by the chat's title. It shows when the chat was last used, and "Working" or "Waiting for your OK" while the chat runs or waits on an approval, or "Snoozed until <time>" while it sleeps, in place of its preview. A click opens that chat, and the top bar's heading names it as its bot and title. Each row has a More button (⋯) beside it, shown on hover or focus in place of the time, and turned off while the connection is not online.

The row's menu opens at the pointer on a right-click, with no desktop menu over it, and under ⋯ from ⋯, the Menu key or Shift+F10. It is drawn by the same popup menu as the [bot menu](./bot-menu.md), with an icon on each item, in four groups: "Pin" (or "Unpin") and "Snooze" (or "Wake" on a snoozed chat), then "Rename…" and "Rename with AI", then "Export as Markdown", "Export as JSON", "Export as HTML" and "Copy link", then "Archive" and "Delete…". The row of a bot's own chat, its Bot Chat as the roster names it, holds only the second and third groups. The first item takes focus. The arrow keys, Home and End move, Escape closes the menu onto the row's ⋯, and Tab closes it too. Snooze opens a submenu beside the menu, on hover, click, Enter or ArrowRight, with "In 1 hour", "In 3 hours", "This evening" (6 PM), "Tomorrow" (9 AM) and "Next week" (Monday 9 AM), soonest first, each with the time it ends at its right end. A choice already past, or ending the same minute as a sooner one, is left out. Each choice is named by its label alone, and the time is its description. Click, Enter or ArrowRight opens it with the first choice focused, and hover opens it without moving focus. ArrowLeft or Escape closes it back onto Snooze.

Pin, Snooze and Wake move the row at once and keep focus on its ⋯ in the new place. A snooze says "Snoozed until <time>" under the list, and Wake says "Woke <name>". A snooze is kept by Highwing, not Hermes, in `snoozes.json`, so it survives a restart, and the row comes back under its group on its own when the time comes. A chat that waits for your OK is never hidden under Snoozed. Rename turns the title into a "Chat name" field: Enter saves, and Escape or leaving the field cancels. Rename with AI asks Hermes, with `llm.oneshot` and the chat's own model, for a title from the chat's first and latest messages, and saves it as Rename does. While it waits the row says "Naming…", and when it lands the list says "Renamed to <title>". An export saves the chat and says "Saved <file name>" under the list for five seconds. Archive takes the row out of the list at once and says "Archived <name>" there. Delete asks first, in a dialog that names the chat, with Cancel and Delete, and says "Deleted <name>". Escape or Cancel closes it onto the row's ⋯. When the archived or deleted chat was the open one, the pane moves to its bot's own chat, unstarted if it has none, and focus moves to the next row. A refusal shows its words under the row. A bot with no chats says "No chats yet." The column follows the open chat to another bot, picked with the bot picker. It is hidden below 680 px of window width.

## Sub-features

- `chat-list-rows` lists a bot's chats by title when the bot is opened.
- `chat-list-open` opens a chat picked from the list, marks its row current, and shows its thread.
- `chat-list-asking` says "Waiting for your OK" on the row of a chat that waits on an approval while another chat is open.
- `chat-list-runs-in` runs a message in a picked chat as its bot, in that chat's session, and leaves the other chats as they were.
- `chat-list-heading` names the open chat in the top bar's heading.
- `chat-list-empty` says "No chats yet." for a bot with no chats, with the New chat button still in the top bar.
- `chat-list-rename` renames a chat from its menu, in the row and in the top bar. Escape and leaving the field send nothing.
- `chat-list-export` exports a chat as Markdown from its menu with the keyboard, saves the file, and names it under the list. The More button never opens the chat.
- `chat-list-offline` turns every More button off while the connection is down.
- `chat-list-archive` archives a chat that is not open from its menu. The row leaves, the open chat stays, and the list Hermes sends after leaves it out.
- `chat-list-archive-open` archives the open chat. The pane shows the bot's unstarted chat, and a message there makes its Bot Chat.
- `chat-list-delete-cancel` opens the Delete dialog and closes it with Cancel and with Escape, sending nothing.
- `chat-list-delete` deletes the open chat after the dialog, with the chat's generation, and the pane moves as for an archive.
- `chat-list-delete-refused` says why a chat whose reply still runs can't be deleted, under its row, and asks Hermes nothing.
- `chat-list-bot-chat` offers no Pin, Snooze, Archive or Delete… on a Bot Chat's row.
- `chat-list-pin` pins a chat under "Pinned", with "Sessions" over the rest, and unpins it, after which the headings go.
- `chat-list-right-click` opens the row's menu at the pointer on a right-click with no desktop menu over it, under ⋯ from the Menu key and Shift+F10, and walks it with the keys.
- `chat-list-snooze` walks the Snooze submenu with the keys, snoozes a chat under "Snoozed" with no call to Hermes, keeps it there across a restart, and wakes it.
- `chat-list-snooze-hover` opens the submenu on hover without moving focus, closes it when the pointer moves on, and snoozes a pinned chat out of Pinned and wakes it back.
- `chat-list-retitle` renames a chat that is not open with the title its model suggests, saying "Naming…" while it waits.
- `chat-list-retitle-unsupported` says a Hermes without `llm.oneshot` can't suggest a title, and renames nothing.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Turn on chat mode from the switch at the top of the left column. Click the bot picker at its right and pick a bot. Its chats show in the Chats column.
- Click a chat's row, or move to it with Tab and press Enter.
- Right-click a row, or hover it, or Tab to its ⋯, and press ⋯. Arrow keys, Home and End move through the menu, ArrowRight opens Snooze's choices, and Enter picks.

## Gotchas

- An ARIA snapshot does not print `aria-current`. Use `current button "<title>"`.
- A row's name is its title alone. The ARIA snapshot prints the rest of the row after the name: the time, then the state words or the preview.
- A snooze choice's name is its label alone, such as `menuitem "Tomorrow"`. The time beside it is its description, which an ARIA snapshot does not print, and it moves with the clock, so no recipe names it.
- The seeded approval is never answered. The gateway has no handler for a seeded chat's `approval.respond`.
- The time on each row is counted from the machine's clock, so a seeded chat reads some days old.
- `called` is checked again when the steps end, so a count of 0 for a call the run later makes fails. Prove a cancel in a run of its own.
- The scripted gateway takes a `rename`, `archive`, `delete` and `sidebar {pinned}` mutate and `session.export` for a seeded chat. Its export holds only the title and the messages. An archived chat leaves its stored list and a deleted one every list, as Hermes does.
- The status line says "Archived <name>", "Deleted <name>" and "Woke <name>", so `absent` of a chat's name fails while it shows. Count the row's button instead.
- `run` deletes `snoozes.json` from the user-data directory before it launches, so a run starts with no chat snoozed even under a name used before. `relaunch` keeps it.
- The Chats column is in the page only in chat mode, and hidden below 680 px. A run in bot mode, or with a narrower `--size`, has no `navigation "Chats"`.
