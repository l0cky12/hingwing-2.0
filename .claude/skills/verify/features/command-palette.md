# Command palette

Ctrl+K (Cmd+K on a Mac) opens a sheet at the top centre of the chat window: a "Search chats, bots and actions" field over a list in three groups, Chats, Bots and Actions. With nothing typed, the list holds the open bot's chats that wait on an approval, then its newest chats without the open one, then every bot, then the actions on offer now. Typing narrows it to the chats whose title or preview holds every typed word, the bots whose name does, and the actions whose label does. A word at the start of a word ranks above one inside a word, and ties go to the newest chat. A chat found by its preview shows the preview under its title. Up and Down move through the list and wrap at both ends. Enter opens the chat, opens the bot, or runs the action, and closes the sheet at once, before Hermes answers, so Ctrl+K opens it again while a bot is still opening. A pick main refuses before it sends anything, an open while it saves a new connection or a pick sent for a connection that is no longer live, brings the sheet back with its query and main's reason in place of the list. A pick Hermes refuses leaves the sheet closed. A chat row says "Working" or "Waiting for your OK" at its right, or when it was last used. Typing also searches the words of messages: the first letter reads the five newest chats of every bot, and a Messages group lists each message that holds every typed word, its chat as the title, its bot at the right and the message under it. Under the messages a row says how far the search has read: "Searching messages…", "Search older messages", which reads the next five chats of every bot and keeps the sheet open, or "Searched all messages" (disabled). Picking a message opens its chat, loads earlier turns until its turn shows, scrolls to it and marks the typed words in it, in the chat already open too. Nothing matching reads "No matches". Escape, Ctrl+K again or a click outside the sheet closes it and gives focus back to where it was. The actions are New chat, Stop (while the open chat runs), Open Settings (with a saved connection, while Settings is closed), Reconnect (while the connection is blocked) and Start Hermes (while Hermes is not running).

## Sub-features

- `palette-open` opens the sheet with Ctrl+K, its field focused.
- `palette-chat` opens a chat by a word of its title.
- `palette-preview` lists a chat by a word only its preview holds, with the preview under the title.
- `palette-no-matches` says "No matches" for words nothing holds.
- `palette-bot` opens a bot by its name.
- `palette-new-chat` runs New chat for "new", above any chat that holds the word.
- `palette-close` closes with Escape or Ctrl+K and puts focus back in the composer.
- `palette-keys` wraps Up and Down at both ends, and keeps the window's own shortcuts, such as Ctrl+N, from acting behind the sheet.
- `palette-messages` lists a message by a word only its text holds, under Messages with its chat and bot.
- `palette-older` reads the next chats of every bot from "Search older messages", then says "Searched all messages".
- `palette-landing` opens a picked message's chat at its turn, older than the turns the chat opened with, and marks the typed words.
- `palette-refused` closes the sheet at once for a pick Hermes refuses, leaves it closed with focus in the composer, and opens nothing.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Press Ctrl+K (Cmd+K on a Mac) anywhere in the chat window. It does nothing while the first-run screen or another dialog is open. It works in Settings, as [Settings](./settings.md) shows.
- Type a few letters, move with Up and Down, and press Enter. A click on a row picks it too.

## Gotchas

- No step reads the `landed` highlight. Read it in the shot. `apps/desktop/test/palette.test.tsx` covers the Messages rows and the search's states, and `packages/hermes/test/hub.test.ts` what each step reads.

- No run reaches main's own refusal of a pick, as [Settings](./settings.md) says. `apps/desktop/test/palette.test.tsx` covers which refusals bring the sheet back.
- The helper has no step that reads `aria-selected`. Read `[selected]` in a shot's `.aria.txt`.
- An option's name is its title alone. The ARIA snapshot prints the rest of the row after the name: the state words or the time, an action's keys, and a matched preview.
- `wait unstarted` passes on Research's own empty Bot Chat too, since the fixture gives Research none. `called session.create '{}' 0` and the top bar's heading tell the new chat apart.
- With words typed, the open chat is listed like any other. Only the empty field leaves it out.
- Stop, Open Settings, Reconnect and Start Hermes are not in these runs: a fixture run has no saved connection, and its chat is not running when the palette opens. `apps/desktop/test/palette.test.tsx` covers when each is offered.
