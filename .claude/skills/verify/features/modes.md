# Bot mode and chat mode

The window is one 288 px column and the chat in both modes, and the heading of that column is a switch. In bot mode, the default, it reads "Bots" and the column is the Bots column. A click on it opens a menu with "Bots" and "Chats", the current one checked. "Chats" turns on chat mode: the open bot's chats take the column under a switch that reads "Chats", and the chat, its composer text, its scroll and a running turn stay as they were. "Bots" in the same menu turns bot mode back on. The `< >` button at the right end of the column's foot, after the Connectors button, flips straight to the other mode with one click. It is named "Switch to Chats" in bot mode and "Switch to Bots" in chat mode, and focus lands on the new column's toggle. The top bar still names the open chat's bot. Main keeps the mode in `mode.json` in Electron's `userData` directory, so the next launch opens in the mode last picked. A file it cannot read opens in bot mode.

In chat mode the right end of the column's heading is the bot picker, a button named "Bot: <name>" for the open bot, or "Choose a bot" when the roster does not list the open chat's bot. It opens a menu named "Bots" that lists the roster as the Bots column does: the default bot first, as "<name>, default bot", then each section. The open bot is checked. A pick opens that bot, as a click in the Bots column does, and the chat list follows it. A pick of the checked bot opens it too, which is chat mode's way to a bot's unstarted chat. A refusal shows its words under the heading. While the roster is not listed the picker is turned off and says why.

## Sub-features

- `modes-switch` turns chat mode on and off from the switch's menu, with focus back on the switch.
- `modes-toggle` flips the mode with the foot's toggle in both directions, from the pointer and the keyboard, in the full and the compact Bots column, with focus back on the toggle.
- `modes-relaunch` opens the next launch in the mode last picked.
- `modes-keys` drives the menu from the keyboard: Enter opens it on the checked item, the arrow keys, Home and End move, Enter picks, Escape and Shift+Tab close it onto the switch, and Tab out closes it. A second click on the switch closes it. End in the menu leaves the thread where it is.
- `modes-follows-bot` lists the open bot's chats in chat mode, keeps the draft in the box across the switch, and makes a new chat in that bot.
- `modes-bound` switches both ways on a Hermes that reaches one profile.
- `modes-layout` lays both modes out at each window width, with and without the preview pane, and in forced colors.
- `modes-picker` drives the bot picker from the keyboard and the pointer: it opens on the checked bot, the arrow keys, Home and End move, Escape and Shift+Tab close it onto the picker, a pick opens the bot and the list follows, and a pick of the checked bot opens its unstarted chat.
- `modes-picker-draft` keeps a draft in a bot's chat across picks of other bots.
- `modes-picker-refused` shows Hermes's refusal under the heading and keeps the chat, and turns the picker off on a Hermes that cannot list its bots.
- `modes-picker-keys` keeps PageUp, PageDown and End in the open picker from scrolling the thread, and fits a long bot name on the heading's line.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Click "Bots" at the top of the left column, then "Chats" in the menu.
- Click "Chats" at the top of the left column, then "Bots" in the menu.
- Or Tab to the switch, press Enter, move with the arrow keys and press Enter.
- Or click the `< >` button at the bottom right of the left column.
- In chat mode, click the bot's name at the top right of the left column, then a bot in the menu.

## Gotchas

- A run's mode carries over to the next run with the same name. A recipe that expects bot mode at launch needs a fresh name.
- `wait bots` passes in either mode, so it does not prove a switch has happened. Wait on the destination's landmark.
- At 679 px and below the switch is hidden in both modes, so `click button Bots` fails there. Switch at a wider size first.
- `focused button Chats` right after `click menuitemradio Chats` passes because the window gives focus to the new switch. The one that was clicked left the page with its column. The toggle works the same way: the window focuses the new column's toggle after a click on the old one.
- No step checks End in an open menu. `pinned` only says whether the thread is at its end, and End must leave a scrolled thread where it is. Check it with a one-off Playwright script on `fixtures/long-turn.jsonl`: send the request, wait for the approval, scroll `#scroll` up and read `scrollTop`, open the menu, press End, then check that focus is on the last item and `scrollTop` has not changed. Without the menu's `stopPropagation` the thread jumps to its end. For the picker, press PageUp and PageDown as well, with the thread scrolled to each end.
- The picker closes, with focus back on it, when a roster refresh takes the focused item or the whole listed roster out of the open menu. No fixture refreshes the roster while a run waits, so no recipe drives this. It is proven by reading `usePickMenu` in `renderer/chat/menu.ts`: an effect closes the menu whenever focus is not inside it.
- A pick shows no refusal until Hermes answers, and a refusal that answers an earlier pick is dropped once a later pick was made.
