# Edit and delete a group chat

Each row under Group chats in the Bots column has a menu, as a bot's row does: Open and Edit…, then Delete… after a separator. A right-click opens it at the pointer, and the ⋯ at the row's right end, Shift+F10 and the Menu key open it under the ⋯. Escape and Tab close it and give focus back to what opened it. Edit… opens "Edit group chat", with the group's name in its Name field, focused and selected, and the members with their faces, which no control changes, over "Hermes can’t change who’s in a group.". Save stays off while the name is blank, the same as before once trimmed, or longer than the 200 characters Hermes takes. Enter or Save renames the group in Hermes (`groups.rename`), and the dialog closes with focus back on the row, which shows the new name, as does the open group's top bar. Escape and Cancel close it and send nothing. Delete… opens "Delete Launch plan?", which says Hermes ends the group chat and deletes its messages and can't bring them back, with Cancel focused. Escape and Cancel keep the group. Delete asks Hermes to delete it (`groups.disband`), which stops any member answering first. The row is busy until Hermes answers, then leaves the list, and focus moves to the group below it, the one above, or the first bot. A window showing the group moves to the bot it was opened over. A refusal is said in the column's alert line and the row stays. While Highwing is not connected, Edit… and Delete… stay focusable and say "Highwing isn't connected to Hermes right now." instead.

## Sub-features

- `group-edit` opens the menu from ⋯, by right-click and by Shift+F10, each focusing Open and giving focus back on Escape, cancels Edit… with Escape, keeps Save off for the same name padded with spaces, and renames the open group with Enter.
- `group-delete` opens Delete… by right-click, from ⋯ and from Shift+F10 with End, keeps the group through Escape and Cancel, then deletes the open group: its row leaves, the pane moves to Ada, and focus lands on Ada's card.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Right-click a group under Group chats, or point at it and press ⋯ at its right end.
- Or give a group row focus with Tab and press Shift+F10 or the Menu key.
- Choose Edit… to rename the group, or Delete… to delete it.

## Gotchas

- The dialog's button is `Delete` and the menu item is `Delete…`, so `click button Delete` never hits the menu.
- A group's ⋯ is `data-group-more` and its row `data-group`, apart from a bot's `data-more` and `data-bot`, so a group and a bot with the same id never share a focus target.
- The fake gateway never refuses a delete. The refusal words (`peer_unreachable`, `room_stop_pending`, `room_disbanding_retry`, a -32601 from an older Hermes) are proven in `packages/hermes/test/groups.test.ts`, and the window puts any refusal in the column's alert line.
- The blocked Edit… and Delete… show only while the link is down, and a `--drop-after` redial lands about 1 s later, too soon to open a menu on. `apps/desktop/test/bot-menu.test.ts` proves the words.
