# Group chats

The plus in the Bots column's header opens a menu: New chat with the open chat's bot, New group chat…, a rule, then New bot…. New group chat… opens a dialog with a Name field, a Search bots field over a list of the bots Hermes would seat, each with a real checkbox, and a tally that names the picked bots ("3 picked: Ada, Sam and Juno"). Create group chat is off until the group has a name and 2 to 6 bots. It makes the group in Hermes once (`groups.create`) and lists it at once under Group chats, between the default bot and the roster's sections, with its members' names as the second line. A row is not a control yet. On a Hermes without group chats, while the link is down, or with fewer than 2 bots to seat, New group chat… stays focusable and says why.

## Sub-features

- `plus-menu` opens the menu from the plus with the pointer or the keyboard, moves through it with the arrow keys, Home and End, closes it with Escape onto the plus, and hands focus back to the plus when a dialog it opened closes.
- `group-create` fills the dialog, makes one group under one id, and lists it under Group chats with its members' names.
- `group-seats` offers only the bots Hermes would seat, keeps the picked ones through a search, keeps Enter in the search from sending, and makes a group on a Hermes that is not running group chats now.
- `group-unsupported` blocks New group chat… with words on a Hermes that has no group chats, which is asked `groups.capabilities` once and nothing more.
- `group-forced` draws the menu, the dialog's checkboxes and the Group chats section in forced colors.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Press the plus in the Bots column's header, or Tab to it and press Enter, then pick New group chat….

## Gotchas

- The dialog's search box and the sidebar's Search bots button share the label "Search bots", so `type "Search bots" …` finds two and fails while the dialog is open. Click `textbox "Search bots"` and `press` the letters.
- A blocked row is `aria-disabled`, which Playwright treats as disabled, so `click menuitem "New group chat…"` waits until the timeout. Reach it with the arrow keys.
- `called` is checked again when the steps end, so `called groups.create '{}' 0` early in a run fails once a later step makes a group.
- The dialog's `showModal()` makes the rest of the page inert, so a step that clicks outside it waits until the timeout.
- A group row is not a button yet. Opening a group and chatting in it come with the next change.
