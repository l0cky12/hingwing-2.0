# Group chats

The plus in the Bots column's header opens a menu: New chat with the open chat's bot, New group chat…, a rule, then New bot…. New group chat… opens a dialog with a Name field, a Search bots field over a list of the bots Hermes would seat, each with a real checkbox, and a tally that names the picked bots ("3 picked: Ada, Sam and Juno"). Create group chat is off until the group has a name and 2 to 6 bots. It makes the group in Hermes once (`groups.create`) and lists it at once under Group chats, between the default bot and the roster's sections, with its members' names as the second line. On a Hermes without group chats, while the link is down, or with fewer than 2 bots to seat, New group chat… stays focusable and says why.

A group row opens the group in the chat column. Its ⋯, a right-click and Shift+F10 open the group's menu, which [Edit and delete a group chat](./edit-and-delete-a-group.md) covers. In an open group the top bar names the group and shows its members' faces and names in place of New chat and Copy link, and the box reads "Message the group, or type @ to ask specific bots". A message goes to every member, or to the members it `@`-mentions. Each reply is drawn under the message it answers, with the member's face and name over it, and an `@handle` of a member is set in sans. While a member answers, the thread ends in "Sam is thinking", the row's second line says the same in green, Stop stands before Send, and the line under the box says "Sam is still answering. If you send now, Sam’s reply won’t be posted." Stop ends that turn, which then reads "Sam was stopped." `@` in the box offers the group's members and puts in their handle. The box keeps its draft per group. Attachments are off. Ctrl+N still starts a chat with the bot the Chats column lists.

## Sub-features

- `plus-menu` opens the menu from the plus with the pointer or the keyboard, moves through it with the arrow keys, Home and End, closes it with Escape onto the plus, and hands focus back to the plus when a dialog it opened closes.
- `group-create` fills the dialog, makes one group under one id, and lists it under Group chats with its members' names.
- `group-seats` offers only the bots Hermes would seat, keeps the picked ones through a search, keeps Enter in the search from sending, and makes a group on a Hermes that is not running group chats now.
- `group-unsupported` blocks New group chat… with words on a Hermes that has no group chats, which is asked `groups.capabilities` once and nothing more.
- `group-thread` opens a group, sends to it, shows each member's reply and who is answering, stops a member, and sends again.
- `group-mention` offers the members under `@`, keeps a group's draft while another chat is open, and starts a new chat with Ctrl+N while a group is open.
- `group-not-running` keeps Send off in a group on a Hermes that is not running group chats, and says why.
- `group-forced` draws the menu, the dialog's checkboxes, the Group chats section and an open group in forced colors.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Press the plus in the Bots column's header, or Tab to it and press Enter, then pick New group chat….
- Click a row under Group chats to open that group.

## Gotchas

- The dialog's search box and the sidebar's Search bots button share the label "Search bots", so `type "Search bots" …` finds two and fails while the dialog is open. Click `textbox "Search bots"` and `press` the letters.
- A blocked row is `aria-disabled`, which Playwright treats as disabled, so `click menuitem "New group chat…"` waits until the timeout. Reach it with the arrow keys.
- `called` is checked again when the steps end, so `called groups.create '{}' 0` early in a run fails once a later step makes a group.
- The dialog's `showModal()` makes the rest of the page inert, so a step that clicks outside it waits until the timeout.
- A group row's accessible name is "<group>, group chat with <members>", and its second line is its description, so `click button "Launch plan, group chat with Ada, Sam and Juno"` opens it whatever the second line says.
- `wait group-thinking` holds as soon as any member thinks. To rest on a later turn, first wait with `said` for the reply before it, as the recipe above does with Juno's.
