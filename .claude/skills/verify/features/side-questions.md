# Side questions

The user types `/btw <question>` and sends it, also while a turn runs. A card headed "Side question" opens above the message box with the question, "Waiting for the answer" and the quiet line "The answer will show in the chat." When Hermes answers, the card leaves and the question and its answer join the thread after the turn that was current when the answer came. The thread draws them as an article headed "Side question", with the question as a quiet line, the answer in the same Markdown as a reply with its own Copy button, and the quiet line "Not saved with the chat. Later replies won't see it." Answers read in the order they came, each with its own question. A failure shows Hermes's error words in the error color, with the question kept. An answer to a side question this window never asked, as when another client asks one in the same chat, joins the thread too, with the question Hermes repeats. Close and Escape work on the cards only: each card has a close button, and Escape in the box, on Send or Stop, on Attach files or Dictate, or on a card closes the newest card. A card closed while waiting never enters the thread. A card still waiting when the chat is read again stays a card that says its answer was missed. A `/bg` prompt is unchanged: its card is headed "Background prompt", keeps its answer, and says "Not part of the chat." Highwing keeps the answers in its memory of the chat, never on disk, and Hermes's stored chat never holds them, so the model never reads them. They are gone when Highwing drops its copy of the chat or restarts.

## Sub-features

- `sides-ask` sends a typed `/btw` line as `prompt.btw` with the words alone, and opens a waiting card that says "The answer will show in the chat.", also mid-turn.
- `sides-answer` takes the card away when the answer comes, and draws the question and its answer in the thread after the turn current at that moment, in the order the answers came. The turns stay as they were. Mid-turn the answer usually sits after the running turn, and the reply keeps streaming above it, as a relay reply does.
- `sides-place` keeps each answer with its turn through a rewind and a rebuild, as the Gotchas say.
- `sides-close` removes a waiting card with its close button and returns focus to the box. Its answer never enters the thread.
- `sides-escape` closes the newest card with Escape in the box, on Send or Stop, on Attach files or Dictate, or on a card, when the slash menu or an edit does not take the Escape. Escape never takes an answer out of the thread.
- `sides-leave` moves focus to the box when a card leaves for the thread while focus is on its close button.
- `sides-elsewhere` draws an answer to a question another client asked in the thread, with Hermes's copy of the question, or "Asked in another window" when Hermes sends none.
- `sides-failed` shows Hermes's error words in the thread in the error color, with the question kept.
- `sides-lost` keeps a card that was waiting when the chat was read again, saying "The answer was missed while the chat reconnected."
- `sides-bg` keeps a `/bg` prompt's card above the box, with its answer and "Not part of the chat."

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Type `/btw` and a question in the "Message Hermes" box, and press Send.
- Read the waiting card above the box, then the answer in the thread. Click the card's × or press Escape in the box to close the card while it waits.

## Gotchas

- Do not press Escape before sending a `/btw` line. The slash menu is open only while the box holds `/` and a name with no space, so `type` with a whole line never opens it, and the Escape closes the newest card.
- `count region "Side question"` counts the cards above the box, and `count article "Side question"` counts the answers in the thread. `said` looks in the thread only, so `said "It turns on"` proves the answer left the card.
- The focus recipe puts focus on the "why?" card in the 0.8 s between the two answers, so add no step between `said "It turns on"` and `focused button "Close side question"`. If the answer wins that race, that `focused` step fails and the run can be repeated.
- An answer stays with its turn while Hermes still shows rows Highwing knows for that turn, archived ones after `/compress` included. If a rebuild leaves the turn out and Hermes offers "Show earlier turns", the answer goes first and stays first after the earlier turns load. Otherwise it moves to the newest turn, as the rebuild in [Chat commands](./chat-commands.md) shows. `/reset` empties the chat, so its answers sit first. A rewind moves the answers of the turns it cuts to the newest turn it keeps, so they stay above the turn the edited message starts.
- A tall answer that lands mid-turn while the thread follows its end sits at the bottom, and the reply streams above it. Scrolled up, the jump control says "New steps below" when an answer lands.
- `fixtures/btw.jsonl` holds no failed or lost answer and no `/bg`. `sides-failed`, `sides-lost` and `sides-bg` are covered by `apps/desktop/test/sides.test.tsx`, and `/bg` live by [Chat commands](./chat-commands.md). `sides-place` is covered by `packages/hermes/test/reduce.test.ts`.
- Closed cards are kept per chat in the renderer, so a card closed in one chat stays closed after a switch to another chat and back. No recipe here switches chats yet.
