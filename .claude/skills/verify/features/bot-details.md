# Bot details

When the open chat belongs to a listed bot, the top bar draws that bot's face and name as a pill inside its heading. The heading's accessible name stays what it was, such as `Hermes Rae · Bot Chat`, and the pill is the button "Rae details". Clicking the pill, pressing Enter or Space on it, or pressing Ctrl+Alt+B (Cmd+Shift+I on macOS) opens the bot's details over the right side of the chat, under the top bar. The same keys close it, as do Escape inside it and its Close details button, and focus goes back to the pill. The panel reads the bot with `profiles.describe`, and edits name, description, persona and picture in place with the Edit dialog's calls and checks. Save sends only what changed, reads the bot again and stays open, and Escape still closes the panel after it, whether Save was clicked or Enter pressed. A rename shows in the sidebar row, the heading and the panel. A refusal shows in Hermes's words in the panel. The tab row for Routines, Memory and Library is not drawn while Details is the only section.

## Sub-features

- `bot-details-click` opens the panel from the pill, filled from Hermes, with no tab row, and closes it back to the pill.
- `bot-details-keys` opens and closes it with the shortcut, Enter, Space and Escape.
- `bot-details-rename` renames the open bot in the panel, and the sidebar row, the heading and the panel show the new name over the same chat. Escape then closes the panel.
- `bot-details-enter` saves twice with Enter, keeps focus in the field, and Escape closes the panel.
- `bot-details-settings` leaves the panel open under Settings, and Escape closes Settings alone.
- `bot-details-clash` shows a rename made elsewhere in Hermes's words, reloads the fields, and keeps the second Save.
- `bot-details-picture` shows a picture Hermes could not store in its words.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Open a bot's chat and click the bot's face and name in the top bar.
- Or press Ctrl+Alt+B (Cmd+Shift+I on macOS) while a bot's chat is open.

- `--profiles fixtures/profiles/rae.json` lists Rae in New section, with the description "Writes the weekly report", the persona "You write short, plain reports for the team." and revision 2, as [Edit a bot](./edit-a-bot.md) says.

## Gotchas

- A Hermes that cannot describe bots answers `profiles.describe` as an unknown method, and the panel shows "This Hermes can't edit bots from Highwing." No fixture option reaches it with bots listed: `--bound` also stops the window from opening a bot's chat, so the open chat has no bot and the top bar has no pill. `apps/desktop/test/bot-details.test.tsx` covers the words.
- A group chat and a chat whose bot is not listed draw no pill, and the shortcut does nothing there.
