# The bot menu

Each bot in the Bots column, the default bot's card included, has a menu: Open chat and New chat, then Edit…, then Pin to top or Unpin and Hide from list, or Show in list for a hidden bot, then Delete…. The default bot's card has no Pin and no Delete…. Its menu also has Memory… beside Edit…, and [Memory](./memory.md) says what that does. [Delete a bot](./delete-a-bot.md) says what Delete… does. A right-click opens it at the pointer. The ⋯ at the row's right end, Shift+F10 and the Menu key open it under the row's ⋯, or under the whole card for the default bot. The menu is drawn outside the scrolling list, flips or clamps to stay in the window, and closes when the list scrolls or a refresh moves its row. Pin, Unpin, Hide and Show in list write Hermes's own `pinned` and `hidden` flags with `profiles.configure`, so Hermes desktop shows the same. Once Hermes has saved the flag and the column has redrawn, a line at the bottom of the column says what happened and focus follows the bot, or for a hide moves to the next bot still in the list. A refusal shows in the column's alert line and moves nothing.

## Sub-features

- `menu-keys` opens the menu by right-click with chat text selected, by Shift+F10 on a row and by the Menu key on ⋯, each exactly once and with no native menu over it, and Escape and Tab give focus back to the opener.
- `menu-marks` pins, unpins from ⋯, toggles the menu with ⋯, hides, shows a hidden bot, shows the hidden default bot back into its card, and hides the card, each with its announcement and its focus move.
- `menu-refused` shows "This Hermes can't change bots from Highwing." for Pin, and refuses New chat up front on a Hermes that cannot switch bots, with no draft opened.
- `menu-actions` opens a new chat with the composer focused, opens Edit… and gives focus back to the row on Escape, and pins through one clash.
- `menu-edges` flips the menu over ⋯ at the bottom edge, and closes it when the list scrolls.

## How to get to it (user POV)

- Right-click a bot in the Bots column, or point at it and press ⋯ at its right end.
- Or give a bot row focus with Tab and press Shift+F10 or the Menu key.

## Gotchas

- Playwright scrolls a control into view before it clicks it, and the list's `scroll` event arrives a frame after the menu opened. The menu closes only once the list's scroll offset differs from the one it opened at, so that late event leaves it open.
- `count menu` with a regex name needs the regex in quotes, or the shell splits `/^More for/` into two steps.
